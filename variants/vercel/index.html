How to generate images with Flux 3 AI

Step 1: Write your prompt

Describe the image you want in natural language. Be specific about style, composition, mood, and key elements for best results.

Step 2: Configure parameters

Select aspect ratio, style presets, and generation parameters. Flux 3 offers flexible controls for fine-tuning output.

Step 3: Generate and refine

Click generate and review results. Iterate with different prompts or use image-to-image to build on existing creations.
